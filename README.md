# Ships

## Brief Description
Ships is a top down PvP RTS (Real-Time Strategy) game where you compete against other players to be the last Ship standing. Each game you start out with your mothership and some money which you use to build out your fleet of spaceships to battle other players in an attempt to destroy their mothership. Last ship standing wins.

## Other Features
### Factions
Before you start the game, you can select which faction to play as. This will decide which ships are available to you in the shop and can help tailor your playstyle. Each faction has a different identity and look to make them all feel a little different.

### Neutral Objectives
On each map, there will be a number of neutral objectives around the map that offer a way to gain some more money and purchase more ships.
#### Neutral Ships
There are a few neutral ships that will spawn at various locations around the map. They will fire back, but when destroyed they will give you a little bit of money.
#### Neutral Patrols
There can be clusters of neutral ships that spawn together in one patrol group. They are much harder to fight together than an individual neutral ship, but when the last ship in the fleet is destroyed, it gives a good amount of money.
#### Supply Drops
Supply Drops will appear on the map every so often and grant every player vision of the area. You then have 60 seconds to get and hold a ship on the area to receive a portion of the payout, evenly split among all players who have a ship on the supply drop location (evenly split, regardless of number of ships). This is the largest payout of any objective, and therefore can cause fights to break out for control of this area.
